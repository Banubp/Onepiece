Nexa · Fintech case study

Confidence before payment

Problem

UPI makes transactions fast, but a balance alone does not tell people what they can safely spend after upcoming commitments.

Product move

Reframe the balance as Safe-to-Spend and connect subscriptions, shared finance, scan-to-pay and contextual AI guidance around that decision.

Design principle

Show the consequence before commitment. Trust should be built into the product model, not added as reassurance copy.

Contribution

Research synthesis, product strategy, flows, UX psychology, interface design, prototyping and system definition.

Booking.com · Travel redesign

One trip, not five bookings

Problem

Travellers experience one journey, while the product splits discovery, stays, experiences, checkout and post-booking into disconnected decisions.

Product move

Create a personalised, journey-aware system for business travellers and luxury explorers that preserves context across the trip.

Key interactions

Destination previews, enhanced search, curated experiences, responsive patterns, hover feedback and animated continuity.

Contribution

Journey mapping, information architecture, interaction design, responsive design system, prototyping and usability reasoning.

Soft · Mental-health product

Support without pressure

Problem

Mental-health tools can feel clinical, demanding or unsafe at exactly the moment a person needs low-friction support.

Direction

A soft visual and conversational system that prioritises emotional safety, privacy, calm check-ins and progressive disclosure.

Principle

Never turn care into performance. The product supports reflection without guilt, streak pressure or visual overload.

Contribution

Research framing, emotional journey, information architecture, interface direction, illustration and interaction concepts.

Onepiece · Portfolio system

Identity through behavior

Problem

Many designer portfolios look like polished marketing sites but do not demonstrate interaction thinking or a repeatable system.

System

A light editorial hierarchy, reusable case and project structures, semantic tokens and a 70×70 interactive mascot.

Mascot logic

Awake on entry, follows pointer and scroll, works on project focus, sleeps after inactivity and sends a plane when contact opens.

Contribution

Workflow, IA, wireframes, visual system, interaction specification, accessible implementation and browser QA.
