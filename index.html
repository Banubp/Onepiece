Nexa · Product case study

Confidence before payment

The problem

UPI makes payment fast, but speed does not answer the question people ask before paying: “Can I safely afford this?” Fragmented subscriptions, shared expenses, and unclear future commitments increase hesitation.

The product move

Reframe the home balance as Safe-to-Spend, then connect recurring commitments, shared finance, scan-to-pay, and guidance around that decision.

My role

Product strategy, user-flow design, UX psychology, interface design, prototyping, and system definition.

Design principle

Show the consequence before asking for commitment. Confidence is a product capability, not reassurance copy.

Booking.com · Product redesign

One trip, not five bookings

The problem

Travel planning is experienced as one journey but handled as disconnected searches, tabs, bookings, and confirmations. Each handoff adds uncertainty.

The product move

Connect inspiration, property evaluation, experiences, and checkout into a journey-aware system that retains context.

My role

Journey mapping, information architecture, interaction design, high-fidelity prototyping, and usability reasoning.

Design principle

Continuity reduces cognitive load. The product should remember the trip the way the traveler thinks about it.
