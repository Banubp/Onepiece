04 / How I evaluate a product

Every product becomes
a case worth investigating.

SHERLOCK is my product-investigation lens. Hover, focus or tap a card to see the question it helps me answer.

SSignal

What symptom, change or opportunity made this problem worth investigating now?

HHow do we know?

Which qualitative and quantitative evidence proves the signal is real?

EExamine the system

How do users, journeys, technology, business and operations affect one another?

RRoot cause

What underlying mechanism—not just the visible symptom—is creating the problem?

LList hypotheses

What competing, testable explanations could account for what we observed?

OOrder investigation

Which test gives the highest information gain for the least risk and effort?

CCreate interventions

Which UI, non-UI, operational or strategic response best addresses the cause?

KKnow it worked

Which metrics, experiments and guardrails prove the intervention created value?
