Clarity after every payment.

A UPI-first money experience that explains what changed, what is safe to spend and what to do next.

Payments became instant. Understanding money did not.

This self-initiated study investigates how a payment habit can become a moment of financial orientation—without turning a fast UPI flow into a heavy banking product.

S

Signal

The symptom that made the opportunity worth investigating.

Observed opportunity

A payment can confirm that ₹2,500 moved, yet leave the user unsure whether bills and goals are still protected.

Financial activity is distributed across UPI apps, bank accounts, cards, investments and informal notes. The transaction is effortless; the mental reconstruction afterwards is not.

How might we help people understand and organise money through transactions they already make—without slowing UPI down?Investigation question
H

How do we know?

Separate evidence from attractive assumptions.

Evidence plan

This is a concept, so the case does not invent interviews or claim shipped impact.

Competitive evidence

UPI leaders optimise reach and speed; digital banks demonstrate clearer budgeting but assume different banking behaviour.

Behaviour to verify

When uncertainty appears, how people split tools, and whether post-payment context creates return value.

Research sequence

Contextual interviews → seven-day diary → card sorting → concept comprehension → task-based usability.

E

Examine the system

Users, journeys, business logic and trust constraints.

Whole-system view

Ananya is digitally confident but financially uncertain; the product must join fragmented signals without pretending all accounts are one bank.

R

Root cause

The mechanism beneath visible fragmentation.

Causal framing

The problem is not missing data. It is missing consequence.

Products organise activity around accounts and services rather than the user’s commitments. People must manually translate a balance into “Can I still afford this?”

Root cause

Activity without orientation

The system records what happened but does not connect it to protected commitments.

Implication

Inspectable guidance

Show inputs, allow correction and avoid judgemental certainty.

Key decisions

Four interventions answer: what do I have, what is safe, what needs attention and what can I do now?

01

02

04
