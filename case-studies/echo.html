S

Signal

A coordination problem disguised as an assistant problem.

Observed failure

A request like “plan a movie” crosses calendars, subscriptions, cinemas, travel, budget and approval.

The user becomes the project manager: transferring context, comparing outputs, catching conflicts and deciding which action is safe.

How can one accountable intelligence coordinate bounded specialists and return one verified outcome?Investigation question
H

How do we know?

Inspect failure, not only successful demos.

Evidence standard

A tool call is not evidence that the real-world outcome happened.

Synthetic scenario

Movie planning exercised conflict detection, approvals, persistence and deterministic replay.

Real connector test

A calendar failure exposed the need for partial outcomes and honest completion states.

Acceptance rule

Every subsystem needs a contract, health check, failure mode and recovery proof before attachment.

R

Root cause

Why more tools do not create trustworthy autonomy.

Causal framing

Planning, acting and verifying are often collapsed into one opaque claim.

Root cause

Authority without boundaries

Read, plan and act permissions blur while workers verify their own claims.

Implication

Trust must be structural

Separate authority, provenance, approvals, replay and independent verification.
